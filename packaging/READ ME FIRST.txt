MASTER TRACK — playtest build
=============================

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
Steam must be running and you must be logged in. That's it — no account needed for
the game itself, and nothing gets installed. The game will show up in Steam as
"Playing Spacewar"; that's expected, it's the test app ID every developer uses.

Unzip the WHOLE folder and keep it together. masterTrack.exe on its own will not
run — it needs the .pck and the data_ folder sitting next to it.


WINDOWS WILL WARN YOU
---------------------
The build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen will say "Windows protected your PC".
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Nothing is wrong; signing costs money and
this is a playtest.


RUNNING IT
----------
Double-click masterTrack.exe.

If something goes wrong, run masterTrack.console.exe instead — same game, but it
opens a console window with the logs in it. Send me what it says.


PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------
One person hosts:
  1. Tick "Use Steam".
  2. Press Host.
  3. Your Steam ID is shown on the menu. Send it to everyone.

Everyone else joins:
  1. Tick "Use Steam".
  2. Paste the host's Steam ID into the "Host ID / IP" box.
  3. Press Join.

You'll land in the lobby together — everyone gets a car in their own colour and
can drive around while the rest arrive. When everyone's in, the host presses
Start Match. One player is picked at random to be the Track Master: they get a
top-down view of the board and build the track everyone else is racing on.


CONTROLS — driving
------------------
  W / Up arrow ....... accelerate
  S / Down arrow ..... brake, and reverse from a standstill
  A D / Left Right ... steer
  Space .............. handbrake
  Shift .............. nitro
  Tab ................ put your car back on its wheels
  Escape ............. leave the match (host only) / back to menu
  `  ................. physics debug overlay

A controller works too.

CONTROLS — Track Master
-----------------------
  Left click ......... place the selected tile
  R .................. rotate
  Right click ........ cancel / hold to look around
  W A S D ............ move the camera


WHAT I'M LOOKING FOR
--------------------
This is about how the cars feel and whether the whole thing is fun. There are no
win conditions, no scoring, and the race doesn't end — that's on purpose. If it
falls over, tell me what you were doing at the time.
